ChatGPT account quotas now load automatically at startup and when accounts are added, so Lite's overview and menu bar can display remaining quota without first opening the Upstreams page.

Quota updates prefer the headers returned by real traffic. Background queries only fill a cold cache, refresh quota that has not been updated for 15 minutes, or check a window after its reported reset time. Manual refresh remains immediate. Concurrent readers share in-flight queries, with at most four account queries running at once; failed queries back off and preserve the last known quota.

Disabled accounts and accounts that need to sign in again are excluded from automatic queries. No configuration changes are required. Updating an already open Lite overview also requires the accompanying Lite event-subscription fix.
