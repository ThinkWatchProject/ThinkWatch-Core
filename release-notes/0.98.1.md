Large requests now wait their turn for the shared request-body budget instead of
immediately returning 429 while later small requests continue to enter. This
addresses the reproducible budget-starvation mechanism investigated in #403;
the reporter's original incident still lacks the response body and runtime
budget data needed to confirm its cause.

Known-size uncompressed uploads reserve space before reading and remain
concurrent. Chunked and compressed requests grow under a single admission turn
to avoid partial-body deadlocks. Decompression can retry once with exclusive
space, releasing its CPU slot while waiting, and returns unused reservations
when the compressed input is discarded.

Admission and budget waits are bounded at 30 seconds. Exhaustion still returns a
retryable 429 and now logs the budget occupancy. Uploads idle for 30 seconds are
rejected and release their budget. The existing memory policy and 256 MiB
per-request limit are unchanged.
